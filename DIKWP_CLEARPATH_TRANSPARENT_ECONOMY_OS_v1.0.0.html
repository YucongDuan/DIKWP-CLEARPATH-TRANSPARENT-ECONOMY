              培训与机会诚信审计

              关键问题不是“课程有没有内容”，而是培训席位与真实剩余付费机会是否匹配。

              反“机会漂白”规则

              • 必须披露未来12个月真实付费名额。
              • 必须披露当前合格供给及预计新增供给。
              • 不得把工具学习人数当成就业需求。
              • 当“剩余付费名额/培训席位”低于10%时，应给出显著晚进入警告。
              • 培训收入超过下游声明机会价值时，不能继续把课程包装成普遍出路。

              透明贡献分配提案

              系统只生成透明提案，不自动付款。

              透明机会经济六层

              1. Transparent demand: funded slots, budget and expiry.
              2. Selective capability transparency: task evidence without private-life exposure.
              3. Transparent saturation: qualified supply and late-entry risk.
              4. Transparent DIKWP contribution: D/I/K/W/P transformations are separately attributed.
              5. Transparent settlement: price, fees, participant share and correction reserve.
              6. Transparent correction: failed forecasts and decisions must be revised.

              禁止偷换

              • Opportunity status is not human worth.
              • Capability evidence is not permanent identity.
              • Full transparency is not total surveillance.
              • Earlier entry is not moral superiority.
              • Model execution ability is not customer access, authority or responsibility.
              • “No current viable path” is not “no future and no value.”
              • Automatic employment, credit, benefit or legal decisions remain prohibited.

              Reference implementation. All market values are user declarations or synthetic presets. The system makes no guarantee of employment, income or future demand. Automatic external-action authority = 0.

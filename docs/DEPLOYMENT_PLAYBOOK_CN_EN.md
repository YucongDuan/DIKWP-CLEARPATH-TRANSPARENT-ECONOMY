# 90-Day Deployment Playbook / 90日部署手册

## Phase 0 - Governance / 治理准备 (Days 1-10)

- Name the accountable institution and decision owner.
- Define prohibited uses.
- Create the appeal and correction process.
- Separate public opportunity data from private person data.
- Choose a refresh interval and expiry rule.

## Phase 1 - Opportunity denominator / 机会分母 (Days 11-30)

For 50-200 routes, collect:

- buyer or funder;
- funded paid capacity;
- current qualified supply;
- expected entrants;
- price, cost, and time to first paid result;
- automation and concentration assumptions;
- training seats marketed;
- source, update date, confidence, and falsifier.

Do not publish a route if the denominator cannot be explained.

## Phase 2 - DIKWP task language / DIKWP任务语言 (Days 31-45)

- Decompose each route into D/I/K/W/P storage and transformations.
- Define evidence stages.
- Identify which transformations require local, regulated, physical, or responsibility-bearing work.
- Create one narrow paid test per candidate route.

## Phase 3 - Participant support / 个体支持 (Days 46-60)

- Let participants declare a Purpose and only necessary constraints.
- Keep health, family, financial, and identity details private.
- Analyze a portfolio, not one fashionable route.
- Provide non-debt bridges for small tests where justified.

## Phase 4 - Paid reality tests / 付费现实验证 (Days 61-80)

A test must have a named buyer, written acceptance, value commitment, loss cap, stop condition, and result receipt.

## Phase 5 - Calibration / 校准 (Days 81-90)

Publish aggregate results:

- routes tested;
- routes closed;
- paid tests obtained;
- median time to first paid receipt;
- realized margin;
- training offers modified or stopped;
- appeals and corrections;
- parameter changes.

## Stop conditions / 停止条件

Stop deployment if:

- the system is used for secret applicant ranking;
- institutions refuse to disclose the opportunity denominator;
- private constraints leak into public records;
- people are denied rights based on route scores;
- no decision changes after the analysis;
- appeals cannot modify records.

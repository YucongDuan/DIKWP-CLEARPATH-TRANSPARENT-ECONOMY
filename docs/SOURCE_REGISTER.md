| GitHub, `Octoverse 2025` | Independent evidence of rapid developer, repository, and AI-project growth | Activity growth does not by itself prove paid demand or business viability |
| U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, software developers outlook | Counterevidence to literal “software jobs are dead” claim | U.S.-specific occupational projection, not a global guarantee |
| World Economic Forum, `Future of Jobs Report 2025` | Broad employer-survey view of job creation, displacement, and changing skills | Survey-based projections, not deterministic forecasts |
| ILO, `Generative AI and Jobs: A Refined Global Index of Occupational Exposure` | Task exposure and transformation framing | Exposure is not the same as job elimination |
| OECD, `A Skills-First Labour Market` | Common skills language, demonstrated skills, and mobility | OECD warns of bias, obsolescence, and worker-protection risks |
| OECD, `Skills in the AI Age` | Automation, new-task creation, productivity, complementarity, and displacement coexist | Aggregate evidence cannot decide an individual route |

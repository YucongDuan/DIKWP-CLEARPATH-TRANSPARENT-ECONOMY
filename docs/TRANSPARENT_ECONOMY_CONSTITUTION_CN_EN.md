# Transparent Opportunity Economy Constitution / 透明机会经济宪法

## Article 1 - The object of judgment / 判断对象

The system judges a route under declared conditions, never the intrinsic value of a person.

系统判断具体路径，不判断人的内在价值。

## Article 2 - Denominator before promotion / 先公开分母再推广

No training, career, or entrepreneurial route should be represented as a general economic opportunity without disclosing funded capacity, qualified supply, counting method, expiry, and uncertainty.

## Article 3 - Paid demand is not attention / 关注度不是付费需求

Views, followers, downloads, waitlists, letters of intent, and verbal enthusiasm are not interchangeable with funded demand.

## Article 4 - Evidence before irreversible investment / 不可逆投入之前先有证据

Large time, debt, relocation, or career decisions require stronger evidence than a small reversible experiment.

## Article 5 - Capability is relational / 能力具有关系性

A capability has economic value only relative to a Purpose, buyer, task, context, acceptance rule, and time.

## Article 6 - Privacy minimum / 隐私最小化

Private constraints may increase support or change routing. They may not be published by default or used to reduce rights.

## Article 7 - No permanent caste / 不得形成永久等级

No DIKWP profile may become a permanent social class, general intelligence score, moral score, or universal wage rank.

## Article 8 - Preserve adverse evidence / 保存不利证据

Failed routes, expired opportunities, dissatisfied buyers, and successful appeals must remain part of the calibration record.

## Article 9 - Training integrity / 培训机会诚信

Training providers must distinguish learning value from downstream paid-opportunity value.

## Article 10 - Human authority / 具名人类责任

Employment, pay, credit, benefit, legal, education, and other high-impact decisions require named lawful authority and due process.

## Article 11 - Correction / 纠错

A wrong consequential record requires restoration, correction, downstream notice, appropriate compensation, and rule or model revision.

## Article 12 - Expiry and reopening / 过期与重新打开

Every closure and every opportunity record has an effective period. New evidence may reopen a route; stale success evidence may expire.

## Article 13 - Agent boundary / Agent 边界

Agents may analyze, compare, and draft. They may not grant themselves authority, execute payments, or make automatic high-impact decisions in the reference core.

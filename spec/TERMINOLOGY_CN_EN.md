# TOEP Terminology / 术语

| Term | Definition | 中文 |
|---|---|---|
| Funded capacity | Paid outcomes or positions backed by a real budget in a declared period | 有真实预算支持的付费容量 |
| Qualified supply | People or systems capable of meeting the acceptance condition | 合格供给 |
| Opportunity denominator | The full entering or competing population relevant to an outcome claim | 机会分母 |
| Saturation ratio | Projected qualified supply divided by funded capacity | 饱和度 |
| First-mover capture | Advantage arising from early control of trust, data, distribution, or switching costs | 先发者占位 |
| Opportunity half-life | Analytical estimate of the speed at which commoditization erodes scarcity | 机会半衰期 |
| Opportunity laundering | Selling a skill as a livelihood route without disclosing the downstream opportunity denominator | 机会漂白 |
| DIKWP transformation | Directed conversion among Data, Information, Knowledge, Wisdom, and Purpose | DIKWP转换 |
| Reality receipt | Evidence that a declared outcome occurred under a declared acceptance rule | 现实收据 |
| Route closure | Time-bounded decision to stop investment in one path | 路径关闭 |
| Reopening condition | Named evidence or changed condition that could reverse a closure | 重新打开条件 |
| Transparent economy | Economy in which demand, supply, evidence, responsibility, and correction are visible without total personal surveillance | 透明经济 |

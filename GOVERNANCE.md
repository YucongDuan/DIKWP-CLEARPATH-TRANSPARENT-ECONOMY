# Governance

ClearPath is governed around five non-negotiable principles:

1. Route decisions must never become human-worth scores.
2. Paid demand, supply denominators, training sales, and uncertainty must be disclosed together.
3. High-impact use requires named human authority, notice, appeal, correction, and expiry.
4. Private constraints remain private by default and may only increase support, not reduce rights.
5. Failed predictions and adverse evidence remain visible and may force model revision or retirement.

Changes to these principles require a public design record, a versioned migration, regression tests, and an explicit explanation of affected users.
